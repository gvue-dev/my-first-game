Alien Tic-Tac-Toe

A solo board game with alien battles. Practice classroom project.

Goal: get three X in a row. Pick a square, then win a short alien battle to turn it into X. Lose the battle and the square becomes O. Three O in a row means the aliens win.

Each side starts with 2 health. A right answer costs the alien 1 health; any other answer costs you 1. The battle ends when someone reaches 0. The alien never takes a separate turn on the board. If the board fills with no line, it is a draw.

You: 2 of 2Alien: 2 of 2

Restart game clears the whole board and any battle in progress.

Classroom practice project. The aliens and the game are fictional.
